# Squads

_Version: 1.0_

A small scale format for Horus Heresy 3rd edition.

## Taking Part

My goal is to get people playing Heresy with a lower barrier of entry.
Don't sweat the small stuff.

It would be awesome to see painted armies on the table, but paint is not required.
Similarly, use what models you have, as long as people can tell what they are.
Third party and 3D prints are fine.

### Allegiance

Ideally we will try to pair up Loyalist vs Traitor battles, but don't let it get in the way of playing games.
Mistaken identity, malfunctioning transponders, or good old back-stabbing could happen in any warzone.

## Event

Each Monday from Nov 2 - Dec 7.
Check the [Arvard Ardboyz Events Calendar](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1_9o3hYoD0H02y6I5YAXE2aPjOY6RuWC7Vvz7mLiDPSU/edit?usp=sharing) in case of updates.

Play as many or as few games as you want.
It is recommended to organize match-ups ahead of time through the club Discord.

Report results to Edward - VPs per side.

You may change your army list between games, just stay within the restrictions.
Please stick to the same Faction.
