# Army Selection

Armies are chosen to a maximum of **750 points**.
Any published units may be used, including Libers, Legendary downloads, Journals. _(As long as they fit within the other restrictions)_.

## Units

- No single unit may cost more than **180 points**, including their additional models, wargear etc.
- **0-1** unit with an Armour Value of **14**.
- **3+** non-Command units.

## Detachments

- **1** Crusade Primary Detachment
  - The _High Command_ slot is removed. The _Special Assignment_ Prime Advantage may still be used to take a High Command unit.
- Auxiliary detachments as normal based on your Command choices, including faction-specific detachments.
- No other detachment types. (Allied, Apex, etc.)

## Factions

### Imperialis Militia

To select two Provenances of War, use _Special Assignment_ to take a Force Commander.

Add this option to the Provenances of War:

> #### Clanfolk Levy
>
> Models with the Irregulars Special Rule and the Cavalry type can control objectives as if they were Infantry.

### Legio Custodes

These units may be taken with a starting size of 3 models:

- Custodian Guard Sodality at 135 points.
- Sentinel Guard Sodality at 120 points.

### Legiones Astartes - Blackshields

Blackshields Oaths of Moment can change scoring rules in a way that is problematic.
If you _really_ want to use Blackshields, discuss with Edward first.
